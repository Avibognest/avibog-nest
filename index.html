About us

About Avibog Nest

Avibog Nest Educational Services is an online tutoring practice for children from Pre-K through high school. Our tagline says it simply: guiding early growth from heart to home.

Our educational philosophy

The Learning Behind Learning

Every answer has a reason behind it. A child who guesses, freezes or says “I just don’t get it” is showing us exactly where understanding has stopped. We call this the learning behind learning, and it shapes every lesson we teach.

Our core message guides the work: understanding first, confidence always. When children understand an idea, they can use it in new places. When they feel confident, they keep trying.

How we teach

A clear process, from first conversation to steady progress

Five steps so you always know where your child is and where we are heading.

  1. 1

    Initial learning assessment

    A friendly, low-pressure set of activities and conversation that shows how your child reads, calculates, explains and approaches problems. It is a starting point for planning, never a test to pass or fail.

  2. 2

    Identifying strengths and learning gaps

    We separate what your child knows well from what is shaky or missing, including foundations from earlier grades. That lets us fix causes, not only symptoms.

  3. 3

    Personalised learning plans

    Clear goals, a sensible order of topics and the right pace for your child, shared with you in plain language.

  4. 4

    Interactive online lessons

    Live lessons planned around your child, with questions, examples, visuals and activities. Children think aloud, try ideas and explain their reasoning.

  5. 5

    Progress monitoring and parent communication

    At Avibog Nest, we believe that parents should be informed about their children’s learning journeys. We provide monthly learning updates to help families understand their child’s progress, strengths, areas requiring additional support and upcoming learning priorities.

Parent progress updates

Monthly Learning Progress Updates

At Avibog Nest, we believe parents should understand what their children are learning and how they’re progressing.

Every month, we provide a personalised learning report highlighting the skills covered, progress observed, areas requiring additional support, and goals for the following month.

Our reports also look beyond correct answers to help parents understand how their children approach learning, respond to challenges, and develop confidence.

Each report includes practical suggestions for supporting learning at home.

What every report includes

The Avibog Nest Monthly Learner Progress Report

  1. 1

    Learner information

    Reporting month, grade, subjects, tutor and sessions completed.

  2. 2

    Monthly learning focus

    The skills and topics we concentrated on this month.

  3. 3

    Progress and supporting evidence

    Where your child is on each skill, with the evidence behind it.

  4. 4

    What I Noticed Beneath the Learning

    How your child approaches learning, responds to challenge and builds confidence.

  5. 5

    Skills requiring additional support

    The areas we will keep working on together.

  6. 6

    Goals for the following month

    Clear priorities for the month ahead.

  7. 7

    Practical home-support recommendations

    Simple ways to support learning at home.

  8. 8

    Tutor’s closing note

    A personal note about your child’s month.

How we describe progress

Each skill is placed on a simple five-point scale.

Beginning Developing Progressing Independent Not Yet Assessed

Every report is written from real lessons, observations and assessments. Each learner’s report is confidential and shared only with their parents or guardians.
